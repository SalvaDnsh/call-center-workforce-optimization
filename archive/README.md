# Original source archive

`original_code.zip` preserves the supplied Python and GAMS code, notebook,
and accompanying output artifacts in their original bytes. The three PDF
reports are available separately under `reports/original/`.

`manifest.csv` lists every source artifact with its SHA-256 checksum. A single
byte-identical notebook duplicate was omitted from the ZIP; its manifest row
points to the retained copy. Original CSV, PNG, and XLSX outputs are historical
artifacts, not the regenerated verified results under `results/`.

The instructor's full assignment PDF and editable Word report copies are not
included in this public-facing package. The model and demand inputs are
documented in the main repository.
